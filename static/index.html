METHODOLOGY

Research principles

What JalQ-R computes, what is genuinely quantum, and what the prototype cannot claim.

01 / MODEL

Exactly-K scheduling

Three fields, four windows, and an equal-volume allocation. Completed events are frozen. The planner uses current observations and forecast scenarios, never realized future weather.

02 / QUANTUM

Expected-stress surrogate

A quadratic Hamiltonian uses RZ/RZZ phase rotations and XX+YY constraint-preserving mixing. Quantum measurements generate candidates; CVaR ranking is performed classically.

03 / GATING

Heuristic invocation

Forecast changes and candidate instability can trigger extra search. This heuristic does not predict quantum advantage and must be evaluated against classical-only and random invocation.

04 / EVIDENCE

Honest evaluation

Exact enumeration is an isolated offline oracle. Quantum-shot and gate-cost metrics are never mislabeled as true quantum speedups or hardware runtime.
